<p>MeshNavarra Utility lets you manage your Meshtastic nodes from your phone, over USB OTG or Bluetooth Low Energy. Unofficial app, not affiliated with the Meshtastic project.</p>

<p>What you can do:</p>

<ul>
<li>Connect the node over USB (OTG cable) or Bluetooth and download its node database.</li>
<li>Browse all mesh nodes as visual cards (favorites first) with battery, signal, hops and last-heard.</li>
<li>Administration: reboot, wipe the node database (keeping favorites), favorite/ignore, remove node and manage admin keys.</li>
<li>Commands: telemetry, position, traceroute, rename node, text messages.</li>
<li>Channel chat with per-message delivery status.</li>
<li>ETSI good practices: radio presets (SFN Spain and stock) applied with safe read-modify-write and backups.</li>
<li>NavaTastic CLI: remote control of repeaters running the NavaTastic/Navarrico firmware via encrypted messages, with a safety gate for destructive commands.</li>
<li>Import nodes by URL and keep your own node cache.</li>
<li>Built-in user manual, in Spanish and English.</li>
</ul>

<p>No network permissions, no ads, no analytics. Free software (GPL-3.0), source code on GitHub.</p>
